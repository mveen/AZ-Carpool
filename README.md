# AZ Carpool Aalsmeer–Alkmaar

Mobile-friendly carpool scheduler for AZ youth football parents. Static site (GitHub Pages) + Firebase Firestore.

Since the modularisation, `index.html` is only a thin page: the app itself lives in the `.js` modules below. Every module has its own test file, and `npm test` runs them all.

## Ritten ruilen (ritten aanbieden en overnemen; vroeger "Ritbeurs")

Wijzigen heeft bovenaan een segment *Wijzigen | Ritten ruilen (n)*. Alleen zichtbaar als de coördinator Ritten ruilen aanzet (Beheer → Berichten → *Ritbeurs*, één schakelaar die direct opslaat).

- **Aanbieden.** Een chauffeur biedt één rit aan (heen of terug), met een kort bericht (max 60 tekens). Intrekken kan zolang niemand de rit heeft. Overgenomen is van de nieuwe chauffeur; die kan hem zelf opnieuw aanbieden.
- **Overnemen: eerste ja wint.** Het overnemen is één batch: aanbod `status: taken` + de rit in `deviations/<dag_richting>` (alleen `driverFamilyId` verandert) + meldingen. `firestore.rules` laat de wijziging alleen toe zolang het aanbod `open` is, dus de tweede batch faalt als geheel. Zitplaatsen en tijdsconflict (< 90 min) zijn alleen een waarschuwing.
- **Ik kan inspringen.** Een back-up meldt een tijdvak (datum, van, tot, "alleen als ik niet al rijd"). Bij een aanbod krijgen alleen back-ups wiens tijdvak de rit dekt een melding; bij een nieuw tijdvak krijgen chauffeurs met een rit in dat tijdvak een melding.
- **Escalatie.** Open aanbod om 18:00 de dag ervoor: één melding "niet gedekt" aan aanbieder en dagcoördinator (`uncoveredAt`, één keer). Het conclusie-appje van 20:00 in Wijzigen is ongewijzigd.
- **Meldingen** zijn nu meldingen in de app (`families/<gezin>/notifications/*`, per gezin leesbaar, vaste id's dus nooit dubbel). Ze hebben `deliverAt` (stille uren, standaard 22:00–06:00, per gezin instelbaar in `families/<id>.ritbeursQuiet`) en `expiresAt` (14 dagen), zodat een latere push-verzender dezelfde documenten kan gebruiken. Echte pushmeldingen vragen een server; dat is nog niet gebouwd.
- **Aan/uit.** `settings/ritbeurs = { on, updatedAt }`. Uit of ontbrekend = uit: schermen en teller verborgen, geen meldingen gemaakt, en `firestore.rules` weigert aanbieden, overnemen, tijdvakken en meldingen. Opruimen (verwijderen) blijft mogelijk.
- **Opruimen/controle** (`checkRitbeurs`, elke minuut en bij terugkeer naar de app): te late aanbiedingen melden; de coördinator verwijdert aanbiedingen van andere weken en afgelopen tijdvakken; ieder gezin zijn eigen verlopen meldingen. Zonder server draait dit alleen terwijl iemand de app open heeft.
- **Code:** `ritbeurs.js` (regels, puur), `ritbeurs-data.js` (database), `ui-ritbeurs.js` (schermen + Beheer-kaart). Tests: `ritbeurs.test.js`, `ritbeurs-data.test.js`, `ui-ritbeurs.test.js`; de regels zijn getest met de Firestore-emulator (`firebase/rules-test/ritbeurs-rules.test.mjs`, handmatig: emulator starten en `node` draaien met `@firebase/rules-unit-testing`).
- **`firestore.rules` is gewijzigd**: plak de nieuwe regels in de Firebase-console → Rules, anders werkt aanbieden/overnemen niet. Service worker cache `az-carpool-v42`.

## Afwijkend weekrooster (aanvragen, advies, akkoord)

Een ouder geeft per dag de tijden van de eigen dochter door voor deze of volgende week (Wijzigen → weekformulier). De dagcoördinator van die weekdag ziet de aanvraag, krijgt een advies voor de auto's en neemt dat over of deelt zelf in. Het vaste rooster blijft gelden tot er akkoord is.

- **Altijd aan.** Er is geen schakelaar meer en geen oude situatie. Elk gekoppeld gezin met een eigen dochter (niet Flex) gebruikt de functie. Het oude document `settings/weekrooster` wordt niet meer gelezen of geschreven; het mag in de database blijven staan (er wordt niets verwijderd). Oude aanvragen met `test: true` (uit de testperiode) blijven geldig.
- **Gegevens (alleen additief).** `weekRequests/<week>_<dag>_<gezin>` met `weekKey, day, familyId, rides, heen, terug, status (pending|direct|accepted|withdrawn), createdAt, updatedAt, [decidedAt, decidedBy], [test]`. Daarnaast op `deviations/<Dag>_<richting>` drie optionele velden: `times` (afgesproken eenmalige tijden, '' = rijdt die kant niet), `selfBy` en `selfAt` (een wijziging van iemand die die dag niet beslist). Er wordt nergens een reden of vrije tekst opgeslagen.
- **Wie doet wat.** "Rijdt niet mee" geldt direct. Een dagcoördinator die voor zijn eigen dag deze week tijden wijzigt, krijgt die direct doorgevoerd (volgende week blijft een aanvraag). Elk gekoppeld gezin mag de aanvragen van andere gezinnen lezen (alleen tijden): ouders zien ze als korte regel bij de dag. De dagcoördinator van een dag (anders de echte coördinator) beslist; `weekrooster-data.js` (`deciderFamilyOf`).
- **Advies.** `weekrooster-impact.js` (`buildAdvice`): *Zuinigst* (via `planShift`) en *Minste gedoe* (bestaande auto's blijven). De reden wordt uit de planning gemaakt. *Eerlijkst* is bewust niet gebouwd.
- **Overnemen en Ongedaan.** `acceptDay` schrijft alle wijzig-shifts én alle aanvragen van die dag in één batch; `undo` zet beide terug. De rules staan dat terugzetten (accepted → pending) alleen de beslisser toe.
- **Zelf indelen / Toch zelf wijzigen.** De ritkaart-editor van Wijzigen staat bij actieve gebruikers in Rooster ("Deze week"). De beslisser opent hem direct; een ouder krijgt eerst de waarschuwing "Dagcoördinator beslist" met de naam van die dag. Zo'n wijziging staat daarna als "Zelf gewijzigd" in het logboek van de dagcoördinator. Geen push, geen inbox.
- **Opruimen.** De browser van de echte coördinator verwijdert, bij de weekwissel, aanvragen van oudere weken (oude testaanvragen ook): `purgeStaleWeekRequests`.
- **Database-regels.** De rules in `firebase/firestore.rules` kijken niet meer naar een schakelaar. Publiceer ze bij het uitrollen van deze wijziging; zolang `settings/weekrooster` op `aan` staat, werkt de app ook met de oude rules. Rules-tests: `firebase/rules-test/weekrooster-rules.test.mjs` (Firestore-emulator).
- **Bestanden:** `weekrooster.js` (pure logica), `weekrooster-data.js` (opslag), `weekrooster-impact.js` (impact en advies), `ui-weekrooster*.js` (formulier, Rooster, advies, editor).

## Run the tests

    npm test

It prints one line per test file (PASS/FAIL with counts), then a rule check, then a total. It exits with an error if anything fails. Tests need Node 18 or newer and no other packages.

### The rule: a module change needs a test change
`tests.lock.json` remembers the version of every module and its test file at the last green run. When a module changed but its test file did not, `npm test` fails and names the module. So every module change comes with a new or updated test in the module's own test file. After a green run, record the new baseline:

    npm run lock

Commit `tests.lock.json` together with the change.

### Render tests (snapshots)
Some tests compare the generated page with a saved copy in `tests/__snapshots__/`. If you change the look on purpose, refresh the copies with `UPDATE_SNAPSHOTS=1 npm test`, read the diff, and keep it only if it is what you intended.

## Day coordinator, conclusie-appje, 1-op-1, Flex
- **Back-up gezinnen (Beheer).** Card *Back-up gezinnen* below *Gezinnen beheren*. *Back-up maken* downloads `az-carpool-gezinnen-<datum>.csv` (semicolon, UTF-8 with BOM, opens in Excel): per family `id`, Ouder, Dochter, Type gezin (vast/flex), Coördinator (j/n), Autocapaciteit, Telefoon 1 and 2, and per weekday the times (`Ma heen`, `Ma terug`) and availability (`Ma rijden heen`, `Ma rijden terug`: *beschikbaar*, *back-up* or empty). *Terugzetten uit bestand…* reads such a file (comma or tab also fine), checks ALL of it first (times hh:mm, capacity 1–9, type, j/n, one coordinator at most, no double ids) and shows either the problems with their line number (nothing is written) or a summary with *Overschrijven* (two taps). Restore **overwrites** the fields above of the families in the file, matched on `id` (if the id no longer exists the family is created again under that id), else on a unique daughter+parent name, else it becomes a new family; families NOT in the file stay untouched, and invite codes, links and pending time changes are never in the file nor touched. It is one batch: all or nothing. The coordinator changes only when the file marks another family with *j* than the current one (the preview warns). Excel dropping the leading 0 of a phone number is repaired. Service worker cache `az-carpool-v15`. No change to `firestore.rules` (only the coordinator writes families and `config/coordinator`).
- **Dagcoördinator (Beheer).** Beheer → *Dagcoördinatoren*: pick one family per weekday. It is stored as family ids in `settings/dayCoordinators`, so name and phone number always come from the family, never from the code. Rooster and Mijn week show the coordinator of the NEXT day, because changes are purged on Friday at 19:00: Mon–Thu "Dagcoördinator morgen: <naam>", Fri until 19:00 nothing, Fri from 19:00 and Sat/Sun "Dagcoördinator maandag: <naam>" (plus a WhatsApp button; nothing when no one is set).

## Periode met andere tijden (holiday, exam week)
**Several periods at the same time.** The coordinator keeps a LIST of periods (Beheer → *Perioden met andere tijden*), because the next collection usually starts while the current period is still running (for example the toetsweek while the herfstvakantie is on). Every period has its own document `periods/<firstDay>` (the first day is the id), its own deadline, its own handed-in times and its own temporary rooster; they only may not share a day (`validatePeriod(raw, others)`: an overlapping period is refused with the name of the one in the way). The filling-in windows may overlap freely. Everything below describes one period; with two periods every card, badge count, overview and rooster view exists per period. The rules for that are in *Several periods* at the end of this section.

Design: "Ontwerp: andere tijden doorgeven voor vakantie en proefwerkweek". It is built in four steps, each with its own tests and a phone check before the next one starts. **Step 1 (done): the coordinator sets the period up.** Nothing changes yet for parents, Rooster or Mijn week.
- **Where.** Beheer → *Perioden met andere tijden*, below *Dagcoördinatoren*: the list of periods and *+ Periode toevoegen*. Fields: name, first day, last day, *Invullen open vanaf*, deadline (date + time). *Opslaan* stores it, *Annuleren* drops unsaved edits, the pencil edits a period, the trash button (tap twice) removes it.
- **Rules** (`period.js`, `validatePeriod`): name required (max 40 characters); first and last day are workdays (Mon–Fri), last not before first, at most 10 workdays (`PERIOD_MAX_WORKDAYS`); filling in opens on or before the deadline day; the deadline is before the first day of the period. Nothing invalid is stored: the first problem is shown in a toast and what was typed stays in the form.
- **Storage.** One document per period, `periods/<firstDay>` (steps 1-3 used a single `settings/period`; see *Several periods at once*): `name`, `firstDay`, `lastDay`, `opensOn`, `deadlineDate` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) and `deadlineTime` (`HH:MM`, local time). Dates are plain strings, so no time zone can shift a day. Separate from `deviations`, so it does not expire on Saturday. `firestore.rules` covers it (`settings/{docId}`: members read, only the coordinator writes). A broken document counts as "no period".
- **Status line** under the intro: no period / *Invullen opent op …* / open until the deadline / deadline passed (only the coordinator can still change times) / period over (`periodPhase`). The "9 van 14 gezinnen doorgegeven" line from the design comes with step 2, when parents can hand in their times.
- **Deadline moment.** *Opslaan* also stores `deadlineAt` (the deadline as a moment in ms). `firestore.rules` compare it with the server clock. A period saved before step 2 has no `deadlineAt`: **open it in Beheer and press *Opslaan* once**, otherwise parents cannot hand in.

### Step 2 (done): parents hand in their times (Wijzigen)
- **Task.** From the day *Invullen open vanaf* every family with a daughter sees a card *Actie nodig: geef tijden door* at the top of Wijzigen, with the deadline, and a red **1** on the Wijzigen tab. ### Back-ups of a period (done)
- **Why.** The coordinator can try things in a period (make or re-plan the temporary rooster, fill in for a parent, change dates) and put everything back, without losing what parents handed in.
- **Where.** Beheer → *Perioden met andere tijden* → under each period (one block per period: name, dates, phase pill, progress bar, folded *Wie heeft ingevuld?* list, folded *Back-ups (n)*; editing opens the form in that period's spot) a folded line *Back-ups (n)*: *Back-up maken*, and per back-up the moment, how many families and shifts it holds, what is different since, a restore button (refresh icon, tap twice) and a trash button. Up to `PERIOD_BACKUP_MAX` (10) hand-made back-ups per period.
- **Content.** Everything of ONE period: the period document, its `periodEntries` and its `periodCars`. Stored as `periodBackups/<firstDay>_<ms>`: `{ periodFirstDay, createdAt, by, auto, period, entries, cars }` (`entries` and `cars` are copies of the documents by id). Pure logic in `period-backup.js`.
- **Restore = everything back.** One batch (all or nothing): the period and every entry and shift of the back-up are written, entries and shifts that exist now but not in the back-up are deleted. What families handed in after the back-up is lost; the second tap says how many families (`changesSince`). A back-up whose dates now share a day with another period is refused (periods never share a day). A broken back-up is never restored.
- **Safety net.** Just before a restore the app stores the current state as `periodBackups/<firstDay>_auto` (one per period; a new one replaces the old one). Restoring that one undoes the restore (and swaps again).
- **Deleting a period** also deletes its back-ups (the second tap says so).
- **Firestore rules (publish `firebase/firestore.rules` again).** New `periodBackups/{backupId}`: coordinator only, also for reading (a back-up holds what families handed in); known fields only. Tested against the Firebase emulator (19 cases: coordinator reads, writes, deletes and restores in one batch; parent and stranger refused for everything; extra, missing and wrongly typed fields refused). Without publishing, *Back-up maken* fails with a permission error.

## Wedstrijden tab
- **Where.** Own tab *Wedstrijden* (between Mijn gezin and Beheer, violet like every match ride). The match carpools used to sit at the bottom of Wijzigen; Wijzigen is now only for one-off ride changes. Mijn week keeps its read-only "Wedstrijden deze week" card; its *Carpool regelen* button opens this tab.
- **Scope.** Every match of the next 29 days (today through 28 days out; `MATCH_HORIZON_DAYS` in `matches.js`) is listed, and the calendar request asks Google for exactly that range (`timeMax`, up to 50 events per team).
- **Only the first 8 days can get a carpool** (today through 7 days out; `MATCH_PLAN_DAYS`, `isMatchPlannable`). Later matches show a small note "Carpool nog niet te plannen. Dit kan vanaf <dag>" (match day minus 7 days). The save handler checks the same rule, so a carpool can never be stored for a match that is too far away.
- **Refresh: once a day, by the first member.** The team calendars are fetched from Google at most once per calendar day (local time). The first member who is in the app that day fetches them and stores the result in `settings/matchCache` (`fetchedAt`, `feedsSig` = the calendar IDs, `partial`); everyone else reads that cache and does not call Google. Trigger (`refreshMatchesIfStale` in `matches.js`, called from `data.js` when the calendars and the cache have arrived, and from `app.js` `checkMatchRefresh` when the app comes to the foreground and once a minute, like `checkWeekRollover`): the cache is not from today, or was fetched for other calendars (Beheer changed; renaming a team does not count), or was partial (one team failed). After an automatic attempt the same calendars are not tried again for 15 minutes (`MATCH_RETRY_MS`), so a failing Google never means a request every minute. Beheer → *Nu verversen* still fetches at once. Two members opening the app at the same moment may both fetch; that is harmless.
- **Nothing to show.** Without any match in the 29 days the tab only shows the info line "Geen wedstrijden de komende 4 weken." and the *Oefenwedstrijd* button. The "set up a carpool" sentence only appears when at least one match can get a carpool.
- **Buttons.** *Ik rij ook* and *Auto toevoegen* sit in one row (`.matchActions`, equal width, fits a phone); *Ik rij ook* disappears while the own car is in. While the car form is open the row is hidden.
- **Vertrek vanaf (v93).** Every car of a match carpool says where it leaves from. Stored on the car as two optional fields: `fromId` (a place id of Beheer, or `AFC` / `ATC`) and `fromText` (free input from *Andere locatie*; never becomes a standard place). Nothing stored = Busstation, so every older carpool keeps working. Pure logic in `locations.js` (`matchFromOptions`, `matchFromChoice`, `matchFromName`, `matchFromFields`). Shown under every car in Wedstrijden, in Mijn week, in the WhatsApp week message (`wa.my.match_van`) and in the description of the calendar feed event ("Vertrek vanaf: ..."). *Ik rij ook* leaves from Busstation.
- **Practice matches (v93).** The button *Oefenwedstrijd* on the info line opens a sheet: AZ team (the calendars of Beheer, never fixed names), opponent (free text), Thuis/Uit, location (Thuis: AFC '34, AZ Trainingscomplex or Andere locatie, so the fixed distances apply; Uit: free text), date and kick-off (must be in the future). Every parent (and the coordinator) can add, change (pencil) and remove (trash, with *Ongedaan*) one. They live in the new collection `practiceMatches/<id>` = `{calendarId, teamLabel, opponent, isHome, location, startMs, createdAt}` (additive; nothing existing changed). `practiceToMatch` (`matches.js`) turns a document into the shape of a calendar match with event id `practice_<id>` and `currentMatchList()` adds them to the calendar matches, so the carpool (`matchCarpools/ev_<calendarId>__practice_<id>`), distances, Mijn week and the agenda feed treat them like any match. The team name follows Beheer through the calendar ID. The summary holds an invisible marker after the separator (`practiceSummary`, read by `analyzeMatch`) so an opponent with a hyphen or a digit at the start is still read right. Changing a match that has a carpool also updates the carpool's copy of the match info; removing one removes its carpool (both come back with *Ongedaan*). `purgeStalePracticeMatches` (data.js) removes one a day after the match. No push notification when one is added (decision). **`firestore.rules` changed: paste the new rules into the Firebase console → Rules**, otherwise adding a practice match is refused. Service worker cache `az-carpool-v93`.
- **Estimated cost.** Under the distance of a match: "± 25,4 km enkele reis · ± € 5,08 carpoolkosten" = km x `KM_COST_EUR` (EUR 0,20, `constants.js`). One way, like the km. - **References.** Mijn week → *Carpool regelen* (`goToMatchCarpool`) opens the tab; Beheer's calendar hint names the tab; `data.js` and `matches.js` refresh the tab when matches, carpools or distances change. `service-worker.js` lists `ui-matches.js` (cache name bumped to v6).

## Help ("?" in the header)
- **What.** The `?` button next to the theme button opens a panel with a search box and a list of topics. A parent types a question ("mijn dochter is ziek") and gets the best articles, opens one, and can jump to the right tab with *Ga naar ...*. Coordinator-only articles (Beheer) show only to the coordinator (`S.canEdit`).
- **How it works.** Plain search over `help-nl.js` in the browser (`help.js`: lower-case, accents removed, filler words dropped, prefix matching, title > keywords > body). No AI, no server, no API key, nothing a user types leaves the phone.
- **Adding or changing an article.** Edit `help-nl.js` (fields are explained at the top of the file), run `npm test`, then `npm run lock`. The test fails if an article contains a link, e-mail address, phone number, long key-like code or password, so a secret can never end up in the help by accident. Write button and tab names exactly as they appear in the app, and update the article whenever a screen changes.
- **Over deze app.** Four articles with `group: 'over'` (achtergrond, privacy, diensten, beheer) are listed under their own heading below the topics. Their text comes from the coordinator's own document; keep it true when the app changes (for example when a service is added or removed). The number of tests in *achtergrond* is exact: `npm run lock` runs all tests and writes the total to `test-count.js` (generated, never edit by hand; upload it with the other files). `npm test` fails with "The help shows N tests, but there are M" until `npm run lock` has been run again, so the number can never be out of date.
- **Panel texts** are in `texts-nl.js` under `help.*`; the `?` icon is `question` in `constants.js`.
- **Later option.** An AI chat on top of the same articles would need a key that must not sit in the app, so it needs a small server. Not built on purpose.

## Texts and languages
Every on-screen text and message is in `texts-nl.js`, as `key: 'text with {placeholders}'`. Code calls `t('key', { name: 'x' })`. Static texts in `index.html` use `data-i18n="key"` attributes. To add a language, copy `texts-nl.js` to `texts-<lang>.js`, translate the values (keep keys and `{placeholders}`), and switch the dictionary in `i18n.js`. Many texts use generic placeholders such as `{p1}`. Look at the Dutch sentence to see what each one is.

5. **Always bump `CACHE_NAME` in `service-worker.js` on every deploy.** Open apps (phone, home screen) check for a changed `service-worker.js` each time they come to the foreground (tab switch, back from another app) and reload themselves once when a new one takes over. ### Cron jobs on the host (`cron/`)
Five jobs run on the mijn.host account (the code is outside the web folder; only the calendar files of the agenda job are written inside it). The fifth, the push job, is only for the test phase: see *Meldingen (testfase)* above. The deploy workflow uploads `cron/` (plus a generated `config.json` with the public project id and Calendar key) to `~/azcarpool-cron/` and runs `run.sh selftest` there; it fails when the host has no Node.js 20+ or an uploaded key does not log in.
- **Back-up** (`bash ~/azcarpool-cron/run.sh backup`, nightly): reads the whole database and writes `~/azcarpool-data/backups/firestore-YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl.gz` (Firestore's own format, one document per line, mode 600). Keeps the last 30 and the 1st of each month for a year. Needs the key `~/azcarpool-secrets/backup-key.json` of a service account with the role *Cloud Datastore Viewer* (read only).
- **Match calendars** (`bash ~/azcarpool-cron/run.sh matches`, daily early morning): does what the first member of the day does in the app (`loadAllMatches` in `matches.js`) and writes `settings/matchCache`. The app then finds a cache from today and fetches nothing itself; if the job is down, the app still fetches by itself. A calendar that fails keeps its earlier matches and the cache is marked partial. Needs `~/azcarpool-secrets/calendar-key.json` (role *Cloud Datastore User*). A test compares its output with the app's.
- **Purge** (`bash ~/azcarpool-cron/run.sh purge`, Friday 19:05): the weekly clean-up that the app used to do by itself when the first member opened it after the week had ended. **The week ends on Friday at 19:00** (`WEEK_END_HOUR` in `constants.js`): from then on the whole app (Rooster, Wijzigen, Mijn week, the calendar feed) shows the coming week, and a new one-off change expires at 19:00 on the Friday of its week (`deviationExpiryMs` in `dates.js`). (1) It logs the rides that were driven (`rideLog/`) with the same app code as the Beheer overview (`rides.js` + `ride-log.js`, copied by the build to `~/azcarpool-cron/app/`; it only holds what those need, no screens), so a ride that ran on a one-off change is counted even if the coordinator never opened the app. (0, first and on its own) It deletes error-log entries (`errorLog/`) older than 30 days. (2) It deletes the changes (`deviations/`) of an older week whose own `expiresAt` has passed, each one only in the version it read (a change that was edited in the meantime stays). If logging fails nothing is deleted. It always counts in Amsterdam time, whatever time zone the host is in, and it is safe to run again at any time. The app keeps its own purge as a safety net, so nothing breaks when this job is down. Uses the same key as the match calendars (`calendar-key.json`, role *Cloud Datastore User*). In DirectAdmin: `5 19 * * 5  bash ~/azcarpool-cron/run.sh purge > /dev/null`. **Keep the old line `5 0 * * 6` as well** (Saturday 00:05, a catch-up): changes made before v90 still carry the old expiry (Saturday 00:00), and the job is safe to run twice. The cron time is the time zone of the host; the job itself always counts in Amsterdam time, so check with `date` over SSH that the host is on Amsterdam time, or the job runs at the wrong hour (it then still works, just later or earlier).
- **Agenda** (`bash ~/azcarpool-cron/run.sh agenda`, cron line every 30 minutes, but the job itself runs at most once an hour and not between 00:00 and 06:00 Amsterdam time: `cron/lib/run-window.mjs`, last run in `~/azcarpool-data/agenda.lastrun`; it reads 9 whole collections per run, and reads count against the free-plan quota): the calendar subscriptions of each family. A family has two links, so for every link it writes `~/domains/azcarpool.nl/public_html/agenda/<secret>.ics` with the next 3 weeks (planning week plus two; from Friday 19:00 on the coming week is the first) of either the rides of the daughter (*Agenda van {dochter}*) or the rides the family drives (*Mijn agenda*, named *Carpool rijden {ouder}*): heen/terug with departure time, route with pickup place and arrival (AFC '34 or ATC) from the rooster, driver, and the time she has to be there / is done. The parents' file names the girls in the car (the family's own daughter too, so her own ride is in both files; a girl with *Rijdt niet mee* is left out, and a car with nobody left is no ride) and the time the first girl has to be there. Matches only when she is in a car of that match (parents' file: when the family drives a car with girls in it). It asks the SAME code as Mijn week (`calendar-feed.js` + `rides.js`, copied by the build to `app/`), so one-off changes, "Rijdt niet mee", the temporary rooster of a period and the standard rooster are all handled as in the app. Times are Amsterdam time, written as UTC. A file whose rides did not change is not rewritten (the time stamp alone is no change); the file of a link that was removed is deleted; a half-written file is never served (temporary file, then rename). It also keeps an `.htaccess` in that folder (calendar content type, no directory listing, no search engines, every rule guarded by `IfModule`). Uses the same key as the match calendars and the purge (`calendar-key.json`, *Cloud Datastore User*). In DirectAdmin: `*/30 * * * *  bash ~/azcarpool-cron/run.sh agenda > /dev/null`. **The web deploy (`deploy-rsync.sh`) excludes `/agenda/`**, otherwise its clean-up would delete the calendar files.
  - **The links** are `https://azcarpool.nl/agenda/<secret>.ics`, two per family: the daughter's calendar (secret in `calendarTokens/<familyId>`, the link that existed before) and the parents' calendar (secret in the new collection `driverCalendarTokens/<familyId>`). Each secret (32 URL-safe characters, 192 bits) is made by the app the first time a family opens Instellingen → Agenda (`ui-agenda.js`, `calendar-feed.js newCalendarToken`); a family that already had the daughter's link keeps it unchanged and only gets the new one. Both documents are `{ token, createdAt }`. The Agenda sheet shows *Mijn agenda* (add to calendar, copy) above *Agenda van {dochter}* (share with her where the phone can share, copy). The job treats both kinds of secret as file names: a secret used twice (over both kinds) is ignored with a warning. Only that family and the coordinator can read it. **There is no "new link"**: `firestore.rules` allows creating it once and no update; only the coordinator can delete a document (in the Firebase console), after which the app makes a new secret the next time that family opens the card. The job accepts only 32 characters from `A-Za-z0-9_-` as a file name (the same rule is in `firestore.rules`), ignores two families with the same secret and ignores a family that no longer exists.
  - **Privacy:** the daughter's file holds her first name, times, places and the driver's name; the parents' file holds the first names of the girls in the cars the family drives, times and places; whoever has a link can read it. Help ("Hoe zie ik de ritten van mijn dochter in mijn eigen agenda?" and the privacy / services articles) says so. Calendar apps fetch the file themselves: Apple about every hour (the file asks for it), Google about once a day.
  - **`firestore.rules` changed (new collection `driverCalendarTokens`): paste the new rules into the Firebase console → Rules**, otherwise the card shows "De agenda-link kon niet geladen worden". Tests: `calendar-feed.test.js`, `cron-agenda.test.js`, `ui-agenda.test.js`; the rules are tested with the Firestore emulator (`firebase/rules-test/agenda-rules.test.mjs`, same way as the Ritten-ruilen rules). Service worker cache `az-carpool-v77`.
- The keys are never in git or GitHub: they are put on the host by hand (folder 700, files 600). Output: one line on success, errors on stderr with exit code 1 (cron mails them). In DirectAdmin use `bash ~/azcarpool-cron/run.sh backup > /dev/null` so only errors are mailed.
- Changes to `cron/` do not need a `CACHE_NAME` bump (the app is not changed).

## Push-meldingen (test en live)

Server-based push notifications for **Ritten ruilen**. Two independent switches, both **off by default**:

| Switch | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Test aan** (`settings/push.mode = 'test'`) | Beheer → Berichten → *Push-meldingen testen* (coordinator only) | Lets the server send test pushes to the **coordinator's own test devices only**. Works whatever the other switch says, so the coordinator can always test. |
| **Push-meldingen aan** (`settings/push.live = true`) | Beheer → Berichten → card *Ritten ruilen*, under the Ritten-ruilen switch | Lets **parents** switch on real pushes on their own phone (Instellingen → *Push-meldingen*); the server then sends them the notifications of their family. |

**What it is.** A Beheer card *Push-meldingen testen* (Berichten), the push switch on the card *Ritten ruilen*, the row *Push-meldingen* in Instellingen (only visible while the push switch and Ritten ruilen are on, for a browser that belongs to a family) and a server job (`cron/push.mjs`). Test pushes are labelled "Test · Ouder van Saar" (the family they were meant for) and a tap opens the app in *Testweergave* as that family; the card also has a preview, five made-up situations and a switch for "it is the middle of the night" (quiet hours). A real push is the plain notification text and a tap opens Ritten ruilen, scrolled to *Meldingen* (`?open=ritbeurs`).

**Who gets what** (`routeEntry` in `push.js`, used by the server):
- Made-up situations, the button *Stuur naar mijn toestel* (`manual: true` in the queue entry) and, while live is off, copies of real notifications → the coordinator's **test devices** (owner `coordinator`), only while *Test aan* is on.
- A **real** notification → the **devices of the family it is for** (`familyId`), only while live is on, only when the notification was made after live was switched on (`settings/push.liveSince`), and only to phones that were switched on before it was made (no old news after switching on). While live is on the coordinator no longer gets test copies of real notifications.
- Nothing else is ever sent. A family without a phone simply gets no push; the notification itself is still in the app.

**Safety, in layers.**
1. `settings/push` holds `{mode, live, liveSince, updatedAt}`. Only the exact word `test` and only `true` switch anything on; a missing or broken document means both off. With both off the server job does nothing at all and the app writes **nothing extra** for anybody. Both switches are always written together, so changing one never wipes the other.
2. The server only sends to real push-service addresses (Google, Mozilla, Apple, Microsoft). `firestore.rules`: only the coordinator creates a test device; a member creates a phone only for the **own family** and only while live is on; a parent may read, change or remove only the phones of the own family; nobody but the coordinator lists the collection.
3. The test screens are hidden for everybody but the coordinator (`push-access.js`).
4. Made-up situations are stored in `testNotifications/` and never touch `offers`, `backupMoments`, `deviations`, `groups`, `families` or a family's own `notifications` (a test checks this for all five).
5. With a switch on, a real notification (Ritten ruilen must be on) also gets an entry in `pushQueue/`, written *after* the real data and best effort: a failing queue never breaks offering or taking over a ride.

**Data (all additive).** `settings/push {mode, live, liveSince, updatedAt}`; `settings/pushKey {publicKey}` and `settings/pushStatus {lastRunAt, mode, live, sent, failed, pending, devices, familyDevices, announced}` (written by the server only); `pushDevices/{id}` (`owner: 'coordinator'` = test device; `owner: 'family'` + `familyId` = a parent's phone; the coordinator's own phone can be both: it keeps `owner: 'coordinator'` and gets a `familyId`); `pushQueue/{path with / as __}` (+ `sentTo`, `failedTo` by the server); `testNotifications/{id}` (vanish after a day).

**The server job** (`bash ~/azcarpool-cron/run.sh push`). While *Test aan* **or** *Push-meldingen aan* is on: makes the key pair once (`~/azcarpool-secrets/push-vapid.json`, mode 600, never in git or in the database) and publishes the **public** half in `settings/pushKey` (parents read it to subscribe); sends due `pushQueue/` entries as described above (never twice: `sentTo`, and the notification `tag`), removes devices the push service says are gone (404/410), gives up on a device after 5 failures, removes expired entries, and reports itself in `settings/pushStatus` (both cards show "Server actief" or a warning). It also announces **"niet gedekt"** (an offer still open at 18:00 the day before) on the server, so that no longer needs an open app; it uses the same rules as `checkRitbeurs` and *creates* the notifications in one commit with the offer change, so the app and the server can never make it twice. The encryption (RFC 8291) and the signature (VAPID, RFC 8292) are written with Node's own crypto, no libraries; the tests check them against the RFC's worked example. A lock file keeps two runs from overlapping. The content of a message is encrypted end to end (the push services only see that a message was sent, not what it says).
- **Quota (v121):** the job does nothing between 00:00 and 05:00 Amsterdam time (an entry that was due goes out from 05:00; the quiet hours of a family are 22:00-06:00 by default anyway), reads `pushQueue` first and only reads `pushDevices` and `families` when there is something in it (the device counts in `settings/pushStatus` are then left out), and only reads `families` and the day coordinators when an offer is really due (*niet gedekt*). An idle run costs about 4 reads plus the offers.
- **Cron:** while testing and in the first days of live, **every minute**: `* * * * *  bash ~/azcarpool-cron/run.sh push > /dev/null`. **Afterwards every 5 minutes:** `*/5 * * * *  bash ~/azcarpool-cron/run.sh push > /dev/null`. Same service account key as the other jobs (`calendar-key.json`, *Cloud Datastore User*). Reads per run with a switch on: about 6 plus one per due entry; with both off: 1.
- **Order of the first run:** (1) paste the new `firestore.rules` into the Firebase console, (2) deploy the app, (3) the cron line is there, (4) Beheer → Berichten → *Push-meldingen testen* → **Test aan** → within a minute "Server actief" appears and the key is published, (5) tap *Meldingen aanzetten op dit toestel* on each test device (Android: Chrome; Mac: Chrome, Edge or Firefox; **iPhone only works with the app on the home screen**), (6) choose a family under *Ik ben…* and play a situation (see `docs/TESTSCRIPT-MELDINGEN.md`), (7) when satisfied: switch **Push-meldingen aan** on the card *Ritten ruilen*, tell the parents to open Instellingen → Push-meldingen on their phone (also in the Help), watch the number of families on the card.
- If `push-vapid.json` is lost the job makes a new pair and publishes it; then each phone must be switched on again.

**Testing it (checklist for real devices).** The automated tests cannot reach the real push services, so this part is by hand: `docs/TESTSCRIPT-MELDINGEN.md` for the test devices. For the live part, with two phones of two different families (or one phone and the coordinator's): switch live on; switch Push-meldingen on in Instellingen on both; offer a ride as one family and check the other family gets the plain push (no "Test ·") and that a tap opens Ritten ruilen at *Meldingen*; check the coordinator's test phone gets **no** extra copy; switch live off and check nothing arrives; remove the phone in Instellingen and check it no longer receives anything.

**The restriction on the test screens.** `push-access.js`: `COORDINATOR_ONLY = true` keeps *Push-meldingen testen* coordinator only (the only line in the app that decides who sees those screens). The parents' own switch (Instellingen) does not depend on it; it depends on the live switch. A test fails if another file starts to know the flag.

**Privacy.** The app stores the push address of a parent's phone (`pushDevices`, with the family) only when that parent switches it on, and removes it when switched off. The push services of Google (Chrome, Android), Mozilla (Firefox) and Apple (Safari on iPhone) only see that a message was sent. The Help (`help-nl.js`: *Push-meldingen op mijn telefoon*, *Over deze app: privacy en AVG*, *gebruikte diensten*) says so.

**`firestore.rules` changed: paste the new rules into the Firebase console → Rules** (blocks `pushDevices`, `pushQueue`). Tests: the unit tests above plus `firebase/rules-test/push-rules.test.mjs` (Firestore emulator, same way as the Ritten-ruilen rules). Service worker cache `az-carpool-v98` (the worker passes `open` of a push to the app).

## Inloggen (gate) and the error log (v88)
- **What the gate tells a parent.** Looking up the code only needs a session. If the database refuses that (a broken or expired anonymous session in an old browser), the app restarts itself ONCE per tab session (sign out, fresh files, reload) and, if it happens again, says "Je verbinding met de app is niet in orde" and points to the button **Lukt het inloggen niet? Opnieuw beginnen** (same reset by hand). "Dit telefoonnummer hoort niet bij deze code" is now only shown when the database really refused the link (`links/<uid>`): a number that is not on the family's `phoneKeys`, or a code of another family. No network gives "Geen verbinding". A pasted code with spaces or invisible characters (WhatsApp) is cleaned (`cleanCode`).
- **Gemelde problemen (Beheer → Koppelingen).** The app writes short technical entries to `errorLog/<uid>_<0-9>`: time, where (`gate`, `start`, `js`), a code (`link-denied`, `lookup-permission-denied`, `signin-…`), a scrubbed message (`scrub()` removes links, e-mail addresses, invite paths and every run of 5+ digits), app version and a coarse browser name ("iOS Safari 17"). **Never** names, phone numbers, codes or the family. Each browser has 10 slots (a ring) and at most 10 entries per page load, so one browser cannot fill the database. Problems that cannot be sent (no network, no session yet) wait in the phone's `localStorage` and are sent at the next start. Uncaught errors and rejected promises are logged too (browser extensions are skipped). Only the coordinator can read or delete them (`firestore.rules`); Beheer shows the 20 newest, a "Wis alles" button, and a line in "Om te checken" when something was reported in the last 24 hours.
- **30 days.** `cron/purge.mjs` (weekly) deletes entries older than 30 days, first and on its own (a failure there never stops the ride logging). The coordinator's app does the same when the log is first loaded, as a safety net.
- **Deploy (v88).** Changed: `index.html` (passes `signOut`), `service-worker.js`, `constants.js`, `coordinator.js`, `data.js`, `state.js`, `app.js`, `texts-nl.js`, `components.css`, `ui-profile.js`, `ui-beheer.js`; new: `error-log.js`, `session-reset.js`. **Publish `firebase/firestore.rules`** (new `errorLog` rules; without them the log silently stays empty, nothing else breaks) and **copy the new `cron/purge.mjs`** to the host (`npm run build:cron`, then the usual upload). Rules test: `firebase/rules-test/errorlog-rules.test.mjs` (emulator, see the other rules tests).


## Planningslogica (standard rooster and period rooster)

One engine plans a shift (weekday + heen/terug): `planShift` in `planning.js`. `checkPlan` re-checks every result independently; a plan that fails the check is not offered as *Aanbevolen* (and logged with `console.error`).

**Who.** Riders are the non-Flex families with a time in that shift. Drivers are families whose availability for that shift is *beschikbaar* (standard) or *back-up*, in the *Selectievolgorde* (Flex families included when their availability says so; a Flex family is never a passenger). A driver has capacity − 1 passenger seats.

**Wait.** Heen: rider time minus the earliest time in the car. Terug: latest time in the car minus rider time. The spread inside one car may never exceed the gap limit (default 3 hours, inclusive).

**Hard rules.** Every rider in exactly one car; a driver has at most one car; seats are enough; spread within the limit; together-rules are kept when possible. Also hard: *Eigen ouder rijdt mee* (Beheer, Reisvoorkeuren; rule `{type:'ownCar', girlId, direction:'terug'}` in `settings/prefs`, family id = driver id = rider id): on a *terug* shift, when that family's parent drives, her daughter sits in her own parent's car, never in another. The parent is not forced to drive; if she does not drive, the rule does nothing. Where no complete plan exists under the rule, the daughter is reported as unplaced, never put in another car. `checkPlan` rejects a plan that breaks it, `planOptions` drops driver swaps that break it. Manual choices (Rooster, Wijzigen, temporary rooster) are never blocked: after saving, `ownCarWarning` (`rides.js`) shows a soft warning toast (in Wijzigen it replaces the confirmation text and keeps "Ongedaan").

**Order of importance among valid plans.**
1. As few back-up drivers as possible (only when the standard drivers cannot cover the shift: seats or the time limit).
2. As few cars as possible.
3. Lowest sum of *Selectievolgorde* ranks of the used drivers.
4. Least total waiting time.
5. Wishes, in this order: samen reizen, the own parent drives the own child, voorkeur. A wish may cost extra waiting only within its window (`prefWindowMinutes` for samen reizen and voorkeur, `parentPrefWindowMinutes` for the own parent), measured per girl as her own waiting time.
6. Tie: the highest ranked driver takes the earliest-leaving car.

The size of a car or a group never decides who gets which group; time decides, seats only decide whether it fits. The search tries all divisions (with a node cap) and is verified against a brute-force reference on 300 random shifts (`tests/planning.test.js`); real roster cases are in `tests/planning-scenarios.test.js`.

### Reads and the free plan (v121)
Firestore's free plan (Spark) allows **50,000 reads a day** for the whole app (the day ends at midnight Pacific time = 09:00 Dutch time); above that, reads fail until the next day. What was done about it:
- **Offline copy in the phone** (`index.html`, `persistentLocalCache` with `persistentMultipleTabManager`): a listener that is started again within about 30 minutes only costs the documents that changed. Falls back to a plain in-memory cache (the old behaviour) where the browser cannot store it. *Opnieuw beginnen* (`session-reset.js`, `clearOfflineCopy`) deletes Firestore's IndexedDB databases too.
- **Cron:** `push` rests at night and reads only what it needs; `agenda` runs at most once an hour and not at night (see above).
- **Beheer data only when Beheer is opened:** the coordinator's app listens to the ride log, the error log and the period back-ups only after the Beheer tab has been opened this session (`S.beheerOpened`, set in `activateTab`; `syncListeners` in `data.js`). The ride logging is done by the nightly purge job on the server, so nothing is lost; `logPassedShifts` and the error-log clean-up in the app are safety nets that run when Beheer is open.
